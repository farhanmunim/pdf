Frequently asked questions

  • Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your PDFs are read from your device, processed in memory and downloaded back to you. There is no server, no account and no analytics.

  • Is there a file size limit?

    There is no fixed limit. The only constraint is your browser’s available memory, so very large files work best on a desktop browser.

  • Does merging or editing reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied object-for-object and text or images are embedded natively into the original PDF. Nothing is rasterised or recompressed.

  • Which fonts can I use in the editor?

    Helvetica, Times and Courier work fully offline. You can also pick from more than 1,900 Google Fonts; the font file is only fetched from Google when you choose one, and it is embedded into your PDF.

  • Can I open a password-protected PDF?

    Not yet. Remove the password first, for example by printing the file to a new PDF, then merge or edit the unprotected copy.

About PDF Tools

PDF Tools is a small set of free, browser-based utilities for everyday PDF jobs: merging files and adding text or a signature to a page.

Everything runs client-side with pdf-lib and pdf.js. There is no backend, no database and no account.

  • Built by Farhan
  • Design benchmarked against App Blueprint

Privacy

Your files are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

The only optional network request is to Google Fonts, and only when you pick a Google font in the editor. Your theme preference is stored in your browser’s local storage.
