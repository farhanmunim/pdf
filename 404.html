About PDF Tools

PDF Tools is a small set of free, browser-based utilities for everyday PDF jobs: merging files and adding text or a signature to a page.

Everything runs client-side with pdf-lib and pdf.js. There is no backend, no database and no account.

  • Built by Farhan
  • Design benchmarked against App Blueprint

Privacy

Your files are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

The only optional network request is to Google Fonts, and only when you pick a Google font in the editor. Your theme preference is stored in your browser’s local storage.
